# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## General Rule
All business-related expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and supported by an original itemized receipt. Claims without a valid receipt will not be reimbursed except for expenses under $10, which may use a self-declared expense form.

## Submission Deadline
Expense claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days require written justification and manager sign-off, and claims older than 90 days will not be reimbursed under any circumstances.

## Travel Expenses
Economy class airfare is standard for flights under 6 hours; business class is permitted for flights exceeding 6 hours when pre-approved by a director. Hotel accommodation is capped at $200 per night in major cities and $120 per night elsewhere, unless a conference venue requires otherwise.

## Meals
Meal reimbursement while traveling on business is capped at $60 per day, itemized as up to $15 for breakfast, $20 for lunch, and $25 for dinner. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Client Entertainment
Client entertainment expenses require pre-approval from a manager and must list the names and companies of all attendees on the expense claim.

## Reimbursement Timeline
Approved claims are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle, typically within 10 business days of approval.
