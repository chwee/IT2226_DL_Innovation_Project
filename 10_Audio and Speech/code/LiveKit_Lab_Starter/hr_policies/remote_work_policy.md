# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 3-month probation period and hold a role that does not require daily on-site presence (e.g. not front-desk, lab, or hardware roles) are eligible to apply for remote or hybrid work arrangements.

## Hybrid Schedule
The standard hybrid arrangement is 3 days in the office and 2 days remote per week, with Tuesdays and Thursdays designated as mandatory in-office collaboration days for all hybrid staff.

## Fully Remote Arrangements
Fully remote arrangements (5 days remote per week) require director-level approval and are reviewed every 6 months. Employees on a fully remote arrangement must be reachable during core hours of 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in their assigned working time zone.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home office stipend of $300 for eligible remote employees, covering items such as a monitor, chair, or desk. Stipend claims must be submitted with receipts within 60 days of the remote arrangement start date.

## Working Abroad
Employees wishing to work remotely from a different country for more than 10 consecutive working days must obtain prior approval from HR and Legal, due to tax and data-residency implications. Unapproved extended overseas remote work may affect payroll and benefits eligibility.

## Data Security
Remote employees must connect to company systems only via the corporate VPN and must not use public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks for work involving confidential data.
