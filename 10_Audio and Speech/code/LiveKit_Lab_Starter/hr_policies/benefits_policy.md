# Employee Benefits Policy

## Health Insurance
All full-time employees are enrolled in the company group health insurance plan from their first day of employment, covering the employee plus up to 3 dependents. The company pays 80% of the premium; the remaining 20% is deducted from the employee's monthly payroll.

## Dental and Vision
Dental and vision coverage is optional and can be added during the annual open enrollment period in November, or within 30 days of a qualifying life event (marriage, birth of a child, etc.).

## Retirement Contribution
The company matches employee retirement fund contributions up to 5% of base salary. Matching contributions vest fully after 2 years of continuous employment.

## Wellness Allowance
Employees receive an annual wellness allowance of $250 to be used toward gym memberships, fitness classes, or mental health counseling sessions. Unused wellness allowance does not carry forward to the next year.

## Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
All employees and their immediate family members have access to a confidential Employee Assistance Program offering up to 6 free counseling sessions per year on topics such as stress, finances, or family issues.

## Professional Development
Employees may claim up to $1,000 per year toward approved courses, certifications, or conferences relevant to their role, subject to manager approval before enrollment.
