# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited at 1.5 days per completed month of service. Unused annual leave may be carried forward into the next year up to a maximum of 10 days; any balance beyond that is forfeited on December 31st. Annual leave requests must be submitted at least 5 working days in advance through the HR portal and require manager approval.

## Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 14 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any sick leave lasting more than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry forward to the following year and is not paid out on resignation or termination.

## Maternity Leave
Eligible employees (minimum 90 days of continuous service) are entitled to 16 weeks of paid maternity leave. Employees should notify HR at least 8 weeks before the expected delivery date to arrange handover and payroll continuity.

## Paternity Leave
Eligible employees are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.

## Compassionate Leave
Employees may take up to 3 paid days of compassionate leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling).

## Unpaid Leave
Unpaid leave beyond statutory entitlements may be granted at management's discretion for a maximum of 30 calendar days per year, subject to written approval from both the direct manager and HR.
