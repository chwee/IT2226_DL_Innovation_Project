SECTION 1: OVERVIEW OF PUBLIC DENTAL SERVICE TIERS IN SINGAPORE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Singapore's public dental care system is organised into two main tiers:

Tier 1 — Primary Dental Care:
- Polyclinics (SingHealth Polyclinics and National University Polyclinics)
- Community Health Assist Scheme (CHAS) dental clinics (private clinics participating in the subsidy scheme)

Tier 2 — Specialist Dental Care:
- National Specialty Dental Centres (NSCs): National Dental Centre Singapore (NDCS) and National University Centre for Oral Health, Singapore (NUCOHS)
- Restructured Hospital Dental Departments (e.g., Ng Teng Fong General Hospital Division of Dentistry, National University Hospital dental services)

Subsidised basic dental services are available at polyclinics and CHAS clinics. For specialised care, patients are referred to National Specialty Dental Centres (NSCs) such as NDCS and NUCOHS.
Source: MOH — https://www.moh.gov.sg/seeking-healthcare/find-a-facility-or-service/dental-services/

SECTION 2: POLYCLINICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ENTITY NAME: Polyclinics (SingHealth Polyclinics and National University Polyclinics)
PROVIDER TYPE: Public Primary Dental Care — Tier 1
OPERATOR: SingHealth Polyclinics (SHP) and National University Polyclinics (NUP)
NUMBER OF LOCATIONS: 14 polyclinics island-wide as of 2025

CARE LEVEL:
Polyclinics provide basic and primary dental care. They are the first point of contact for subsidised dental services. Complex or specialist cases are referred to National Specialty Dental Centres or restructured hospitals.

SERVICES PROVIDED:
- Dental check-up and examination
- Scaling and polishing (preventive care, removal of tartar)
- Tooth fillings (restorations)
- Simple tooth extractions
- Dental X-rays
- Simple root canal treatment (limited to front teeth)
- Simple crowns
- Simple dentures (replacing 5 teeth or fewer)
- Denture repair and fitting
- Simple pulpectomy
- Patient oral health education
- Fluoride application

NOTE: Cases suitable to be done in the polyclinic are determined on a case-by-case basis. Cases deemed more complex are referred to tertiary institutions. Polyclinic dental services already provide denture fabrication, repair and fitting services; however, complex denture cases that require specialist management are referred to tertiary institutions.
Source: SingHealth Polyclinics — https://polyclinic.singhealth.com.sg/our-clinic-services/dental-services
Source: MOH Parliamentary QA, November 2025 — https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/polyclinic-dental-services-provide-denture-fabrication--repair-and-fitting-services/

SPECIALISATION:
General dentistry only. No specialist services are provided at the polyclinic level. Polyclinics do not have registered dental specialists. Oral health therapists (OHTs) work alongside dentists to perform basic dental services such as scaling and polishing, and fillings for young patients.

APPOINTMENT AND WAITING TIME:
- Dental services at polyclinics are strictly by appointment only, except for emergency cases.
- Appointments can be made via HealthHub (https://eservices.healthhub.sg/appointments) or by calling SingHealth Polyclinics at 6643 6969 (SHP) or via the NUHS App (NUP).
- Median waiting time for non-emergency, basic dental services at polyclinics: approximately 3 months.
- Emergency dental cases are seen on the same day.
Source: MOH Parliamentary QA, January 2025 — https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/waiting-time-for-dental-services-at-public-healthcare-institutions-and-proposal-for-more-polyclinics-to-offer-dental-services/

REFERRAL REQUIREMENT:
- No referral required. Patients can self-book via HealthHub or the NUHS App.
- Polyclinics cannot provide referral letters without first conducting a consultation (examination and diagnosis). X-rays may be required before a referral is issued.
- CHAS cardholders whose private dentist is from a registered CHAS clinic may also be referred directly to NDCS or NUCOHS for subsidised dental services.
Source: SingHealth Polyclinics — https://polyclinic.singhealth.com.sg/our-clinic-services/dental-services

SUBSIDY AND ELIGIBILITY:
- Subsidised dental services are available for Singapore Citizens (SCs) and Singapore Permanent Residents (PRs).
- CHAS cardholders (CHAS Blue, CHAS Orange, Pioneer Generation, Merdeka Generation) receive additional subsidies at polyclinics and CHAS clinics.
- From 1 October 2025: enhanced CHAS subsidies extended to ten preventive dental procedures for CHAS Orange cardholders; increased subsidy caps for seven restorative procedures for PG, MG, CHAS Blue and CHAS Orange cardholders.
Source: MOH CHAS — https://www.moh.gov.sg/managing-expenses/schemes-and-subsidies/chas/

REFERRAL PATHWAY OUT:
- Patients requiring more complex or specialised dental care are referred from polyclinics to the National Specialty Dental Centres (NDCS or NUCOHS) for subsidised specialist treatment.

SECTION 3: NATIONAL DENTAL CENTRE SINGAPORE (NDCS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ENTITY NAME: National Dental Centre Singapore (NDCS)
PROVIDER TYPE: National Specialty Dental Centre (NSC) — Tier 2 Specialist
CLUSTER: SingHealth
LOCATION: 5 Second Hospital Avenue, Singapore 168938 (within Singapore General Hospital campus)
WEBSITE: https://www.ndcs.com.sg
CONTACT: 6324 8802

CARE LEVEL:
NDCS is Singapore's flagship specialist dental centre. It provides multi-disciplinary and integrated oral healthcare for patients across all life stages. It is a tertiary-level, specialist-only centre. Complex dental cases referred from polyclinics, CHAS clinics, and other public hospitals are treated here. NDCS does not replace primary dental care; once a patient's condition is stabilised, routine care is managed by a primary care dentist or family dentist.

SERVICES PROVIDED — CLINICAL DEPARTMENTS AND SPECIALTIES:

1. Department of Restorative Dentistry
   - Diagnoses, treats and manages diseases of the teeth and their supporting structures
   - Includes: Endodontics and Prosthodontics

2. Endodontics
   - Root canal treatment (complex cases)
   - Management of diseases of the dental pulp and periradicular tissues
   - Treatment of injuries and diseases of the dental pulp

3. Prosthodontics
   - Restoration of natural teeth
   - Replacement of missing teeth with artificial substitutes (crowns, bridges, dentures, implants)
   - Diagnosis, treatment planning, rehabilitation of oral function, comfort and appearance

4. Department of Orthodontics
   - Correction of crooked teeth
   - Improvement of occlusion (bite) and facial aesthetics
   - Braces and orthodontic appliances

5. Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery (OMFS)
   - Surgical specialty treating diseases, injuries and defects in the oral cavity, jaws, and face
   - Jaw surgery
   - Dental implant surgery
   - Removal of impacted/wisdom teeth
   - Management of facial trauma and jaw deformities

6. Periodontics
   - Diagnosis, prevention and treatment of gum diseases
   - Treatment of gum disease around teeth and dental implants

7. Paediatric Dentistry
   - Comprehensive oral health care for children from birth to adolescence
   - Management of children with dental anxiety or special needs

8. Special Care Dentistry Clinic (Geriatric and Special Needs)
   - Oral healthcare for elderly patients
   - Oral healthcare for adults with complex needs (physical, medical, developmental, or cognitive conditions)
   - Geriatric Special Care Dentistry Clinic expanded due to ageing population
   - Tele-consult and remote assessments for vulnerable seniors in community or nursing homes

ADDITIONAL SERVICES AND CAPABILITIES:
- Teledentistry
- Digital dentistry using 3D technology and new imaging tools
- SingHealth Duke-NUS Disease Centre collaboration
- NDCS Population Oral Health community outreach programmes
- NDCS Clinic @ HPB (Health Promotion Board building) — satellite clinic

RESEARCH:
- National Dental Research Institute Singapore (NDRIS): translates research discoveries into novel therapies for oral and general health improvement.

EDUCATION:
- Teaching institution for dental specialists, radiographers, oral hygiene therapists, dental technicians, and dental surgery assistants.
- Training for residents undergoing postgraduate specialty training.

SPECIALISATION:
NDCS covers all 8 MOH-recognised dental specialties:
1. Endodontics
2. Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery
3. Orthodontics
4. Paediatric Dentistry
5. Periodontics
6. Prosthodontics
7. Dental Public Health
8. Special Care Dentistry (recognised from 1 July 2025)
Source: MOH/DSAB — https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/recognition-of-special-care-dentistry-as-a-dental-specialty/

APPOINTMENT AND WAITING TIME:
- Median waiting time for specialist services at NSCs: approximately 1 month (33 days) for non-emergency cases.
- Emergency cases are seen on the same day.
- Appointments can be made or changed via: Health Buddy App (with SingPass), HealthHub (https://eservices.healthhub.sg/appointments), or by calling 6324 8802.
- SMS reminder sent 5 working days before appointment date.
Source: MOH Parliamentary QA, January 2025 — https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/waiting-time-for-dental-services-at-public-healthcare-institutions-and-proposal-for-more-polyclinics-to-offer-dental-services/
Source: MOH Parliamentary QA — https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/subsidised-specialised-dental-treatment/

REFERRAL REQUIREMENT:
- Referral required from polyclinic or CHAS dental clinic for subsidised treatment.
- Subsidised patients from polyclinics and CHAS dental clinics requiring specialist or complex dental treatment can be referred for specialist dental care at NDCS.
- NSCs may also receive subsidised referrals from public hospitals and other government bodies such as the Health Promotion Board (HPB).
- Private patients (not seeking subsidies) can self-refer.
- CHAS, MG and PG cardholders enjoy subsidised referrals to NDCS when assessed to be clinically appropriate.
Source: MOH — https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/subsidised-specialised-dental-treatment/

SUBSIDY AND ELIGIBILITY:
- Subsidised treatment available for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents referred via polyclinic or CHAS clinic.
- Private patients are not eligible for subsidised rates.

NUCOHS Dental Clinic @ NTFGH officially opened on 19 March 2025 to support rising demand for oral healthcare in the west of Singapore.

SPECIALISATION:
NUCOHS covers all 8 MOH-recognised dental specialties. It is Singapore's first national multidisciplinary dental centre integrating clinical and research expertise with education. It is co-located with NUS Faculty of Dentistry, enabling collaborative training and research.

Unique distinctions compared to NDCS:
- Only NSC with a dedicated Dental Sleep Medicine Clinic
- Only NSC with a dedicated Orofacial Pain Clinic
- Integrated with NUS Faculty of Dentistry and NUH academic health system
- 3 clinic locations across different regions of Singapore (central, west, and south-west)

APPOINTMENT AND WAITING TIME:
- Median waiting time for specialist services at NSCs: approximately 1 month (33 days) for non-emergency cases (same as NDCS).
- Appointments can be made via: NUHS App (OneNUHS), HealthHub (https://eservices.healthhub.sg/appointments), or by calling +65 6716 2233 or emailing NUHS_Dental@nuhs.edu.sg.
- Patients should arrive 15 minutes before their appointment for registration.
Source: NUCOHS — https://www.nucohs.com.sg/for-patients-and-visitors/appointments
Source: MOH Parliamentary QA, January 2025

REFERRAL REQUIREMENT:
- All patients at NUCOHS are registered as private patients by default.
- Only patients referred by polyclinics and CHAS clinics are eligible for government subsidies when receiving treatment at NUCOHS.
- NUCOHS welcomes patient referrals from general dentists, medical practitioners, as well as specialists.
- Private patients (not seeking subsidies) can self-refer or be referred by any medical/dental practitioner.
Source: NUCOHS First Visit — https://www.nucohs.com.sg/for-patients-and-visitors/your-first-visit
Source: NUCOHS Make a Referral — https://www.nucohs.com.sg/for-healthcare-professionals/make-a-referral

SUBSIDY AND ELIGIBILITY:
- Subsidised treatment available only for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents referred via polyclinic or CHAS clinic.
- CHAS, MG and PG cardholders enjoy subsidised referrals to NUCOHS when assessed to be clinically appropriate.
- Self-referred or privately referred patients are treated as private patients (no subsidy).

EDUCATION AND RESEARCH:
- Co-located with NUS Faculty of Dentistry; trains next generation of oral healthcare professionals.
- NUS undergraduate dental students conduct supervised clinical training at NUCOHS (S.M.I.L.E. Clinic).
- Undertakes translational research in regenerative biology, tissue engineering, clinical trials, and public health research.
- Clinical Research Unit on site.

SECTION 5: RESTRUCTURED HOSPITAL DENTAL DEPARTMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ENTITY NAME: Restructured Hospital Dental Departments (Hospital Dental Clinics / HDCs)
PROVIDER TYPE: Hospital-Based Specialist Dental Care — Tier 2
EXAMPLES:
- Division of Dentistry, Ng Teng Fong General Hospital (NTFGH) / JurongHealth Campus
- Department of Dentistry, National University Hospital (NUH) — dental services via NUCOHS
- Dental services at other public restructured hospitals

NTFGH DENTISTRY CONTACT:
Website: https://www.ntfgh.com.sg/our-services/clinical-services/dentistry
Cluster: National University Health System (NUHS)

CARE LEVEL:
Restructured hospital dental departments provide the complete spectrum of dental services — both general and advanced specialist dental care. Unlike polyclinics, these departments also manage inpatients requiring dental care due to hospitalisation for medical conditions. They have multi-disciplinary teams of specialists and generalists working together.

SERVICES PROVIDED AT RESTRUCTURED HOSPITALS (e.g. NTFGH Division of Dentistry):

General Dentistry:
- Dental check-up, scaling and polishing
- Fillings (tooth restorations)
- Simple and surgical extractions
- Crowns and bridges
- Dentures (full and partial)
- Routine root canal treatment
- Patient oral health education

Specialist / Advanced Services:

1. Orthodontics
   - Dental braces (fixed)
   - Oral habit management (e.g. thumb sucking, forward tongue posturing)
   - Adjunctive orthodontic treatment (extruding/intruding teeth, redistributing space)

2. Periodontics
   - Diagnosis, prevention and treatment of gum diseases around teeth and dental implants

3. Dental Implantology Programme
   - Surgical placement and prosthetic restoration of dental implants
   - Bone augmentation procedures

4. Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery
   - Correction of jaw-size discrepancies
   - Enhancement of bone site and surgical placement of dental implants
   - Management of facial and jaw deformities (e.g. cleft lips and palate)
   - Management of impacted and buried teeth, cysts, tumours and other jaw pathologies
   - Management of trauma to teeth, jaws and/or face as a result of accidents
   - Orthognathic surgery

5. TMJ (Temporomandibular Joint) / Bite Therapy
   - Diagnosis and management of jaw pain/disorders

6. Wisdom Tooth Management
   - Surgical removal of impacted wisdom teeth

7. Inpatient Dental Care
   - Management of dental conditions for patients admitted to the hospital
   - Pre-surgical dental clearance for medical/surgical patients

INTEGRATED CLINICAL PROGRAMMES (NTFGH):
The Division of Dentistry has three integrated clinical programmes with teams of specialists and generalists working together to provide seamless, patient-centric multi-disciplinary care.

REFERRAL SOURCES ACCEPTED:
- Polyclinics
- Other local public and private hospitals
- Singapore Armed Forces (SAF)
- Schools' dental services
- Private medical and dental practitioners
- Regional (overseas) specialist referrals
Source: NTFGH — https://www.ntfgh.com.sg/our-services/clinical-services/dentistry

SPECIALISATION:
Full spectrum general and advanced specialist dental care. Accredited by the Singapore Dental Council as a provider of Continuing Professional Education (CPE). Active in research collaborations with NUS, NTU, Ngee Ann Polytechnic, Peking University, and other institutions.

APPOINTMENT AND WAITING TIME:
- Waiting time varies by hospital and specialty.
- Subsidised patients referred from polyclinics or public hospitals are typically seen within weeks.
- Appointments managed via NUHS App or by contacting the hospital directly.
Source: NTFGH — https://www.ntfgh.com.sg/for-patients-and-visitors/appointments

REFERRAL REQUIREMENT:
- Referral required from a polyclinic or public hospital doctor for subsidised patient classification.
- A subsidised patient is a Singapore Citizen or Singapore Permanent Resident referred to a public restructured hospital without specifying a particular specialist. The hospital assigns a specialist at its discretion.
- Private patients (including those who self-refer or are referred by private practitioners) are treated as private patients and are not eligible for subsidised rates.
- HDCs can partner NSCs (NDCS/NUCOHS) to provide an expanded scope of subsidised services subject to MOH approval.
Source: NTFGH Charges — https://www.ntfgh.com.sg/for-patients-and-visitors/charges-and-payment
Source: MOH — https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/subsidised-specialised-dental-treatment/

SUBSIDY AND ELIGIBILITY:
- Subsidised treatment available for Singapore Citizens and PRs referred from polyclinics or restructured hospitals.
- Patients must not be existing private patients for the same medical condition to qualify for subsidy.

SECTION 7: CARE PATHWAY AND REFERRAL FLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STANDARD CARE PATHWAY FOR SUBSIDISED PATIENTS:

Step 1: Patient visits a polyclinic (self-booked, no referral needed).
Step 2: Polyclinic dentist assesses the patient.
  - If basic care is sufficient: treatment is completed at the polyclinic.
  - If specialist care is required: polyclinic issues a referral letter to NDCS, NUCOHS, or a restructured hospital dental department.
Step 3: Patient attends the NSC or restructured hospital dental department with the referral letter.
  - Subsidised rates apply only if referred by polyclinic or CHAS clinic.
  - Without a referral, the patient is treated as a private patient (full fees apply).
Step 4: After treatment at the NSC is completed or the condition is stabilised, the patient is discharged back to their primary care dentist or polyclinic for routine follow-up.

EMERGENCY PATHWAY:
- Emergency dental cases can be seen on the same day at polyclinics (call 6643 6969 for SHP).
- NDCS has an Emergency Dental Services unit.

ALTERNATIVE PATHWAY (CHAS):
- CHAS cardholders whose private dentist is from a registered CHAS dental clinic can be referred directly to NDCS or NUCOHS for subsidised dental services, bypassing the polyclinic.
- NSCs may also receive subsidised referrals from public hospitals and other government bodies such as the Health Promotion Board (HPB).

SECTION 8: REGULATORY AND GOVERNANCE BODIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Singapore Dental Council (SDC):
- Established under the Dental Registration Act 1999.
- Regulates dental professionals and upholds oral health standards.
- All dental practitioners in Singapore must be registered with SDC and hold a valid Practising Certificate (PC).
- Dental Specialist Register maintained by SDC; only accredited specialists may use specialist titles.
Website: https://www.sdc.gov.sg/

Dental Specialists Accreditation Board (DSAB):
- Accredits dental specialist training programmes.
- Recognises dental specialties in Singapore.
- Currently recognises 8 dental specialties (as of 1 July 2025).
- Supported by the Secretariat of Healthcare Professional Boards (SPB).
Website: https://dsab.healthprofessionals.gov.sg/

Healthcare Services Act (HCSA):
- Regulates all outpatient dental services in Singapore.
- All dental clinics must comply with HCSA licensing requirements.
- Clinical Governance Officer (CGO) must be a fully registered dentist with SDC, holding a valid practising certificate with at least 2 years of full-time experience.
Source: HCSA — https://www.hcsa.gov.sg/outpatient-services/outpatient-dental-service/

Ministry of Health (MOH):
- Sets policy, subsidies, and standards for dental care in Singapore.
- Oversees the public dental care system including polyclinics, NSCs, and restructured hospitals.
Website: https://www.moh.gov.sg/

SECTION 9: KEY DATA POINTS FOR RETRIEVAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: How many polyclinics provide dental services in Singapore?
A: 14 polyclinics as of 2025.
Source: MOH Parliamentary QA, January 2025.

Q: What is the waiting time for a dental appointment at a polyclinic in Singapore?
A: Approximately 3 months for non-emergency cases. Emergency cases are seen on the same day.
Source: MOH Parliamentary QA, January 2025.

Q: What is the waiting time for specialist dental care at NDCS or NUCOHS?
A: Approximately 1 month (median 33 days) for non-emergency cases.
Source: MOH Parliamentary QA — https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/subsidised-specialised-dental-treatment/

Q: Do I need a referral to visit NDCS or NUCOHS?
A: Yes, a referral from a polyclinic or CHAS dental clinic is required to access subsidised treatment at NDCS or NUCOHS. Without a referral, patients are treated as private patients and pay full private rates.

Q: Do I need a referral to visit a polyclinic dental service?
A: No. Patients can self-book a dental appointment at any polyclinic via HealthHub or the NUHS App without a referral.

Q: What dental services are provided at polyclinics?
A: Basic dental care including scaling and polishing, fillings, simple extractions, dental X-rays, simple root canal treatment (front teeth only), simple crowns, simple dentures (up to 5 teeth), denture repair, and simple pulpectomy.

Q: What are the 8 recognised dental specialties in Singapore?
A: Endodontics, Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Orthodontics, Paediatric Dentistry, Periodontics, Prosthodontics, Dental Public Health, and Special Care Dentistry (recognised from 1 July 2025).
Source: DSAB/MOH — https://dsab.healthprofessionals.gov.sg/specialist-training/list-of-recognised-specialties/

Q: What is the difference between NDCS and NUCOHS?
A: Both are National Specialty Dental Centres (NSCs) providing all 8 dental specialties. NDCS is under SingHealth and is located at the SGH campus. NUCOHS is under NUHS and operates across 3 locations (Kent Ridge, NTFGH, Alexandra Hospital). NUCOHS has unique multidisciplinary clinics including a Dental Sleep Medicine Clinic and an Orofacial Pain Clinic, and is co-located with the NUS Faculty of Dentistry.

Q: Can a private dentist refer a patient to NDCS or NUCOHS for subsidised care?
A: Only if the private dentist is from a CHAS-accredited clinic and the patient holds a valid CHAS card. Otherwise, the patient is treated as a private patient.

Q: What is CHAS and how does it relate to dental care?
A: The Community Health Assist Scheme (CHAS) enables Singapore Citizens to receive subsidies for dental care at participating private dental clinics. From 1 October 2025, enhanced subsidies apply for ten preventive and seven restorative dental procedures. CHAS dental clinics can also refer eligible patients to NDCS or NUCOHS for subsidised specialist care.
Source: MOH CHAS — https://www.moh.gov.sg/managing-expenses/schemes-and-subsidies/chas/

Q: What dental services do restructured hospital dental departments provide that polyclinics do not?
A: Restructured hospital dental departments provide advanced specialist services including full dental implantology programmes, orthognathic (jaw) surgery, management of facial trauma and jaw deformities, TMJ treatment, inpatient dental care, and multi-disciplinary care for complex medical-dental cases. They also accept referrals from SAF, schools' dental services, and overseas practitioners.

Q: Where does NUCOHS operate?
A: Three locations — main campus at 9 Lower Kent Ridge Road (NUS campus), NUCOHS Dental Clinic @ NTFGH (Jurong East), and NUCOHS Dental Clinic @ Alexandra Hospital.
